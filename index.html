About

I am an independent researcher with a background in numerical optimization, mathematics, computer science and teaching. My current work focuses on machine learning methodology, feature selection, validation, high-dimensional data and biomedical applications.

Research interests

  • Machine learning methodology
  • Feature selection and dimensionality reduction
  • High-dimensional data analysis
  • Model validation and performance variability
  • Numerical optimization
  • Biomedical data analysis
  • Scientific computing

Current and recent research

  • PCA-τ with ANOVA/FDR Rescue
    Hybrid dimensionality-reduction and feature-selection strategy for preserving discriminative low-variance principal components.
  • AUC variability under repeated resampling
    Study of the empirical variability of ROC AUC estimates in Parkinson's disease voice datasets under different train-test allocations.
